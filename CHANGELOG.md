# Worm Simulator: what's new

Each version has a number and a name. Play any version at `wormsimulator.com/v1/`,
`wormsimulator.com/v2/`, and so on. The newest version is at the top.

## Coming next: V2 · Underground

### New
- The Start screen shows which version you are playing: V2 · Underground.
- **Deeper leaves.** The fallen leaves are 2 to 5 cm deep now, more like a real forest floor in
  fall. They pile up deeper in hollows, against logs and trees and where the wind blew them, and
  they are thinner on bumps.
- **Your worm travels inside the leaves**, not on top of them. The leaves above it fade so you can
  still see it, down in the dark, damp old leaves where it crawls.
- **The Action button.** When your worm can do something special, a big button shows up in the
  bottom right corner with a picture and a word. Press Space, or tap the button on a touch screen.
- **Real size for good.** After V1, your teacher chose to keep True size, so the World scale
  button is gone. Every leaf, rock and tree is as big as it really is next to your worm. (V1 still
  has the button.)

## V1 · True Size (2026-10-06)

### New
- **World scale:** a new button on the Start screen. Pick Classic, Hybrid or True to see the
  forest at a different size next to your worm.
- **True size is the new normal.** Leaves, mushrooms, rocks, ferns, the fallen log and the trees
  are as big as they really are next to a 15 cm earthworm. One oak leaf can cover most of your
  worm!
- Tree trunks, rocks and the fallen log are solid now. Your worm slides along them instead of
  crawling through them.
- When a tree or rock gets in the way, the camera moves in closer so you can still see your worm.
  If a tree is right behind you, the camera looks down from above.
- Leaves that show up near your worm grow in gently instead of popping in.
- The Start screen shows which version you are playing: V1 · True Size.
- The speed test now tells you which world scale it used.

### Fixed
- Leaves shake much less when your worm crawls under them.
- Leaves no longer stick into the ground.

## Before V1

The game was built and tested before students joined as designers:
- a forest floor full of fall leaves that the worm crawls under and over;
- graphics settings (Low to Ultra) so it runs smoothly on school Chromebooks;
- a speed test that picks the best setting for your Chromebook.
