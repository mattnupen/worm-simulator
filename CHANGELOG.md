Each version has a number and a name. Play any version at `wormsimulator.com/v1/`,
`wormsimulator.com/v2/`, and so on. The newest version is at the top.

## Coming next: V3 · Predators

### New
- The Start screen shows which version you are playing: V3 · Predators.
- **Three steps on the Start screen: Learn, Play, Vote.** Do them in order. Each one unlocks
  after the one before.
- **Learn: Who eats worms?** Meet the animals that hunt worms, with real photos: the robin, the
  woodcock, the star-nosed mole, the shrew, the garter snake, the American toad and the red-backed
  salamander. Watch a robin hunt, feel the ground shake, and make guesses.
- At the end of the lesson, the game reminds you to fill in this week's form in Canvas.
- While you crawl, a question at the top helps you think: which animals in this forest hunt
  worms, and how could your worm sense them and get away?
- **Settings** (on the Start screen and when you pause) has Sound on or off and the Graphics
  choices. The Speed test button is gone from the Start screen.

### Because you voted
- You picked **predators** as the next topic, so V3 is all about them. This week you get to design
  how a predator should work in the game. The best design gets built into V4.

### Fixed
- On the highest graphics setting, little mushroom clumps near your worm used to shrink and
  vanish as it crawled up to them. They're gone now, so nothing disappears in front of you.

## V2 · Underground (2026-10-08)

### New
- The Start screen shows which version you are playing: V2 · Underground.
- **Deeper leaves.** The fallen leaves are 2 to 5 cm deep now, more like a real forest floor in
  fall. They pile up deeper in hollows, against logs and trees and where the wind blew them, and
  they are thinner on bumps.
- **Your worm travels inside the leaves**, not on top of them. The leaves above it fade so you can
  still see it, down in the dark, damp old leaves where it crawls.
- **The Action button.** When your worm can do something special, a big button shows up in the
  bottom right corner with a picture and a word. The word tells you what Space will do. Press
  Space, or tap the button on a touch screen.
- **Real size for good.** After V1, your teacher chose to keep True size, so the World scale
  button is gone. Every leaf, rock and tree is as big as it really is next to your worm. (V1 still
  has the button.)
- **Dig down!** On open ground the button says **Dig down**. Press Space (or tap the button) and
  your worm noses down into the ground. The camera swings round to the side, and you see a slice
  of the forest floor, like a cake cut open.
- **The layers of the forest floor.** From the top: **Leaf litter** (this fall's bright leaves),
  **Old leaves** (dark, broken leaves with white fungus threads and tiny roots), **Humus** (black
  and crumbly), **Topsoil**, and **Subsoil** (a pale grey band, then rusty brown, with stones).
  The names are at the end of the slice nearest your worm. If that end is off the screen, the
  names wait at the edge of the screen.
- **Point to dig.** Push the arrow keys, W A S D or the joystick the way you want your worm's head
  to go. Your own tunnel is the fastest way to travel. New soil is slower, and the deep layers are
  the slowest. Stones and big roots block you, so slide along them.
- **Castings.** As your worm digs, it leaves castings (worm poop) in its tunnel. Where it eats
  through old leaves and humus, the tunnel wall gets a rim of dark crumbs.
- **Look closely** for a snail shell, a beetle, a spider, a puffball and moss with red stalks. They
  don't move yet. Your vote decides which forest-floor life we add next.
- **Two views underground.** **Wide** shows the whole slice. **Close** follows your worm. Press
  Space to switch: the button shows the view you will switch to. The game remembers your pick on
  this computer.
- **Come up.** When your worm's head is near the top of the leaves, and there's room up there to
  crawl out, the button says **Come up**. Press Space and your worm crawls back out onto the
  forest floor. It leaves a burrow opening with a little pile of castings and leaf bits around it,
  called a midden, like a real nightcrawler. Up to 8 openings stay until you close the game.
- **Stuck?** Point back along your worm's body, and it backs up, tail first, through its own
  tunnel.
- The first time you go underground, a card shows the digging controls and a true fact about
  nightcrawlers.
- Mushrooms and puffballs are for looking at, not eating: the hint cards now say so.
- If your computer is set to reduce motion, going down and coming up is a quick fade through dark
  soil instead of a camera swing.

### Fixed
- **Real tunnels.** When your worm digs, its tunnel is a real hole now. You can see into it, with
  light on the floor and shadow on the roof, and your worm lies down inside it. Castings sit in
  the tunnel as little lumps.
- **Your worm's body stays in its tunnel.** Even after a sharp turn, your worm's whole body
  follows its tunnel all the way round. When it backs up, it goes back the way it came.
- *(These two fixes were made on 2026-10-08, before V2 went out.)*

## V1 · True Size (2026-10-06)

### New
- **World scale:** a new button on the Start screen. Pick Classic, Hybrid or True to see the
  forest at a different size next to your worm.
- **True size is the new normal.** Leaves, mushrooms, rocks, ferns, the fallen log and the trees
  are as big as they really are next to a 15 cm earthworm. One oak leaf can cover most of your
  worm!
- Tree trunks, rocks and the fallen log are solid now. Your worm slides along them instead of
  crawling through them.
- When a tree or rock gets in the way, the camera moves in closer so you can still see your worm.
  If a tree is right behind you, the camera looks down from above.
- Leaves that show up near your worm grow in gently instead of popping in.
- The Start screen shows which version you are playing: V1 · True Size.
- The speed test now tells you which world scale it used.

### Fixed
- Leaves shake much less when your worm crawls under them.
- Leaves no longer stick into the ground.

## Before V1

The game was built and tested before students joined as designers:
- a forest floor full of fall leaves that the worm crawls under and over;
- graphics settings (Low to Ultra) so it runs smoothly on school Chromebooks;
- a speed test that picks the best setting for your Chromebook.
