# V2 · Underground: interface contract between Plan A and Plan B

Written 2026-10-06 by the lead session, before planning. Spec:
`docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-v2-underground-design.md` (approved 2026-10-06; read sections 2
and 11 first, including 11.8 and 11.9).

V2's build is split into two plans that are written in parallel and executed in order on
branch `v2-underground`:

- **Plan A, foundations and surface**
  (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-v2-underground-part-a.md`):
  - version bump;
  - World scale removed;
  - content system;
  - Action prompt and touch Action button;
  - deeper surface litter with the worm buried in it;
  - the perf gates made True-only.
- **Plan B, underground**
  (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-06-v2-underground-part-b.md`):
  - everything in `src/underground/`;
  - the surface/underground mode machine;
  - dig down and come up, the camera, burrow openings and middens;
  - the underground perf run;
  - the closing tasks: gates run, screenshots, CHANGELOG lines, as-built record, version doc.
- **Plan C, Should items** (spec 8.1), is planned later, only once A and B have landed.

Plan B executes after Plan A. Plan B's planner verifies against the current branch (without A's
code), so its tasks must depend on A only through the names below. Where a Plan B task edits a
file that Plan A also edits (`src/main.ts`, `src/game/world.ts`, `src/save/settingsStore.ts`, the
surface materials), it must say so, describe the edit by intent as well as code, and warn the
implementer that Plan A has changed the file first.

## What Plan A provides (exact names; Plan B relies on them)

### 1. Content system (`src/content/`)
- `src/content/sources.ts`:
  - `export type SourceId = ...` (a string-literal union);
  - `export const SOURCES: Readonly<Record<SourceId, Source>>`, where `Source` holds
    `{ title, publisher, url, checked: 'YYYY-MM-DD' }`.
  - Seed it from `docs/codev/research/mn-forest-floor.md`.
  - Include at least: Worm Watch *Earthworm Ecological Groups* (id `wormwatch-ecogroups`),
    Minnesota DNR *Humus Types* (`mndnr-humus`), and Worm Watch *About the Forest*
    (`wormwatch-forest`).
- `src/content/text.ts`:
  - A line is one of:
    - `{ kind: 'ui'; text }`;
    - `{ kind: 'fact'; text; source: SourceId; verified: true }`;
    - `{ kind: 'gameChoice'; text }`.
  - `export const TEXT` is an object of named lines (`as const satisfies Record<string, Line>`).
  - `export function shown(line: Line): string` gives the string the player sees. A gameChoice
    line gets the prefix `"Game choice: "`.
  - A unit test fails on a fact without a known source or with `verified` not `true`. `tsc`
    should reject this too, where the types allow it.
- **Keys Plan B uses.** Plan A creates them all, with this wording:
  - `digDown` (ui) "Dig down"
  - `comeUp` (ui) "Come up"
  - `viewWide` (ui) "Wide view"
  - `viewClose` (ui) "Close view"
  - `layerLitter` (ui) "Leaf litter"
  - `layerOld` (ui) "Old leaves"
  - `layerHumus` (ui) "Humus"
  - `layerTopsoil` (ui) "Topsoil"
  - `layerSubsoil` (ui) "Subsoil"
  - `undergroundHint` (ui) "Point the way to dig with the arrow keys, W A S D, or the joystick.
    Space: switch view, or come up near the top. Esc or P: pause."
  - `burrowChoice` (gameChoice) "real nightcrawlers dig one deep up-and-down burrow. In the game
    you can dig any way you like."

### 2. The Action prompt and the touch Action button (`src/ui/actionPrompt.ts`)
- `export type ActionKind = 'digDown' | 'comeUp' | 'viewWide' | 'viewClose';`
  - `viewWide` means "Action will switch to the Wide view".
  - `viewClose` means "Action will switch to the Close view".
- `export interface ActionPrompt { set(kind: ActionKind | null): void; readonly touchHeld: boolean; dispose(): void }`
- `export function createActionPrompt(root: HTMLElement): ActionPrompt`
- **How it looks:**
  - One real DOM element in the HUD, showing an icon plus the word from `TEXT`.
    - On keyboard devices it also shows a "Space" key chip.
    - On touch screens it is a large button, at least 64 px, at the bottom right, and the touch
      Action press goes through it.
  - `set(null)` hides it.
- **How it behaves:**
  - `set()` is called every frame. When `kind` is unchanged it does nothing: no DOM writes, no
    allocation.
  - Text is at least 18 px with 7:1 contrast. The touch target is at least 64 px. No flashing.
    If the prompt pulses, at most 1 Hz, and not at all under reduced motion.
- **Touch input:** `touchHeld` is true while the touch button is pressed. `main.ts` ORs it into
  `Commands.action`.
  - Keyboard Space already sets `Commands.action`.
  - `Commands.action` stays a held level. Consumers detect the press edge themselves.
- `src/input/actionEdge.ts`:
  - `export function createPressEdge(): { update(level: boolean): boolean }` returns true only on
    the step where `level` goes from false to true.
  - No allocation per call.
- **Icons:** one per `ActionKind`, in `src/ui/icons.ts`.

### 3. Surface heights and litter (`src/surface/litterDepth.ts`)
- `litterDepth(x, z)`: the depth of the loose L layer, in world units.
  - V2 range is about 0.5 to 1.2 u (2 to 5 cm real).
  - Deeper in piles and drifts, thinner in the open and on rises.
- `export function floorHeight(x: number, z: number): number`: the top of the F layer. It is the
  ground under the loose leaves, where a buried worm's belly rests.
- `export function litterTopHeight(x: number, z: number): number`: the top of the loose leaves.
  It is about `floorHeight + litterDepth`, as the leaves are actually drawn.
- **Coherence (spec 8.2):** at the dig-down point, Plan B makes the cutaway's L band exactly
  `litterTopHeight - floorHeight` thick, and puts the cut's F top at `floorHeight`.
- **Names that must not be renamed or removed** (Plan B's dig-allowed check uses them):
  - `rocks()` and `heroLog()` (src/surface/propPlacement.ts);
  - `treeSites()` and `trunkRadiusOf()` (src/surface/treeSites.ts);
  - the footprint queries in `src/surface/obstacles.ts`;
  - `WORLD_HALF`.

### 4. World scale removed
- **Plan B code must not import `src/surface/worldScale.ts`, `scaleSpec()` or `setScale()`.**
  - Plan A decides whether a one-entry table stays.
  - Constants Plan B needs, such as `UNITS_PER_CM`, live in `src/surface/realScale.ts`, with
    `export const UNITS_PER_CM = 3.5 / 15`.
- Plan A removes:
  - the World scale screen and button;
  - `?scale=`;
  - the perf gates' `--scale` option.
- `World.switchForest` may stay or go (Plan A decides). Plan B's underground group is built once,
  at load, separately.

### 5. Settings (`src/save/settingsStore.ts`)
- Plan A removes `scale` from `Settings`. A stored `scale` value is still kept on write, as an
  unknown field.
- `updateSettings(store, patch)` keeps its name and its rule: write a field only when the player
  picked it.
- Plan B adds `undergroundView: 'wide' | 'close'` with the same rule:
  - it is stored only when the player switches;
  - otherwise it reads as `DEFAULT_UNDERGROUND_VIEW = 'wide'`.

### 6. Version and changelog
- Plan A's first task sets `src/version.ts` to `{ number: 2, name: 'Underground' }` and opens
  `CHANGELOG.md` "Coming next: V2 · Underground", adding its own student-facing lines.
- Plan B adds its lines in its closing task.

## What Plan B owns
- `src/underground/`:
  - the slab model and soil grid;
  - the cut-face rendering;
  - stones, roots, life hints and layer labels;
  - worm movement in the plane, with point steering;
  - the mode machine (surface, digging down, underground, coming up);
  - the camera (Wide/Close) and the transitions.
- The surface side of digging:
  - the dig-allowed check;
  - feeding `ActionPrompt.set()` with the right kind each frame:
    - `digDown` on the surface where allowed;
    - `comeUp` underground near the top;
    - `viewWide` or `viewClose` deeper down;
  - burrow openings with middens on the surface.
- Pausing the surface's expensive updates while underground.
- The perf gates' underground run, and the e2e tests for underground.
- The closing tasks:
  - running the perf gates;
  - screenshots in `docs/codev/versions/v02-underground/`;
  - the CHANGELOG lines;
  - the spec's as-built record;
  - the draft `docs/codev/versions/v02-underground.md`.

## Shared rules (both plans)
- Follow CLAUDE.md, `docs/superpowers/visual-quality-brief.md` and the V1 plan's format and
  "Before you start" conventions (`docs/superpowers/plans/2026-10-03-v1-true-size.md`, lines
  1–120).
- Real size only.
- At most 40 draw calls in every view.
- No shader compiles on entering or leaving underground, or on preset changes (checked in e2e).
- No per-frame or per-step allocation.
- Files stay under about 260 lines. `main.ts`, `crawl.ts` and `obstacles.ts` must not grow:
  extract instead.
- Motion smoothness tests are frame-to-frame (rotation, position, reversal) over a deterministic
  seed sweep (handoff lessons).
- Every commit ends with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Opus 5.5 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Always
  `git add` exact files.
